§01 The Stakes

An industry, made by students, on every front.

Gay Gaming Professionals teamed with arcade powerhouse Raw Thrills to ask a simple question: what if the students who'll build the next decade of games had a real budget, real judges, and a real stage — right now, this summer? The answer arrived August 13, 2025.

Three categories. Twenty-six judges from across the industry. $90,000 in cash prizes. Submissions from undergraduate and graduate students at universities across the country. A live awards stream on GGP Twitch with media partner IGN. And a roster of winners — and honorable mentions — that any production company would happily greenlight.

These students rose to the challenge, and submitted phenomenal games, trailers and business strategies. Talent truly is found everywhere — especially when given access and the resources to thrive. — Gordon Bellamy, CEO, Gay Gaming Professionals

§06 A Thank You — In Specifics

The Challenge ran because of the people who built it.

A national competition with $90,000 in prizes, twenty-six industry judges, a live awards stream, media partnership with IGN, and submissions from nine universities does not assemble itself in its first year. It happened because one company underwrote the vision before anyone else.

Founding Underwriter · The Reason This Existed in Year One

Raw Thrills

Every dollar of the $90,000 prize purse — every podium photo, every check into a student's hand, every line of the press release — exists because Raw Thrills underwrote the inaugural Collegiate Games Challenge. Not as a logo placement. As the founding partner that bet on a first-year program built around student talent, before anyone else had to.

Raw Thrills is the dominant U.S. arcade-game studio — Big Buck Hunter, Cruis'n Blast, Halo: Fireteam Raven, Jurassic Park Arcade. Their leadership built the platform on which a generation of arcade culture stood. By backing the Challenge, they extended that legacy directly into the next generation — choosing the future of the medium as deliberately as they shaped its present.

Future supporters: this is the seat Raw Thrills holds. If you believe in students entering the games industry with cash in hand, an industry-quality stage, and a national platform — there is room beside Raw Thrills at the founding table for Year Two. Talk to GGP.
Program manager & brand director

Mabel Chung

Mabel held the thread between sponsors, judges, partners, and the GGP team — the kind of coordination work that becomes invisible only when it's done well. The brand voice you can hear across the press release, the social channels, the awards stream, and this page itself — that's her instinct made shareable. Without Mabel, the day did not happen on time, on tone, or on purpose. Thank you, Mabel.

Christopher Kim

Christopher came UP through GGP's own pipeline — he was a GGP CYSTEM Scholar first, and now he's the one building the next ladder for students behind him. That arc is the entire thesis of this work. As producer, he anchored the operation — student outreach, submissions, the on-campus relationships that turned a press release into actual entries from actual students. USC on the podium in all three categories isn't coincidence — it's craftsmanship. Thank you, Christopher.

She held the second seat next to Christopher through submissions, judging, and the August 13 awards. The Challenge ran cleanly because she was in the room. Thank you, Kalinda.
